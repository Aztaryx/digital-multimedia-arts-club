Welcome to DMAC Welcome to DMAC

Welcome to DMAC! We're a community for anyone who loves making things — whether that's editing video, drawing digital art, taking photos, building websites, or just messing around with new software and hardware to see what it can do. No experience needed, no pressure to be "good" yet. We meet regularly to work on projects together, learn from each other, and share what we've made. If you've ever thought "I wish I knew how to make that," this is where you find out.

Video & Animation

Graphic Design & Digital Art

Web Development

Building websites and web apps — including this one.

Tech & Computer Skills

ADB, benchmarking, computer setup, and getting under the hood of how things work.

Why Join DMAC Why Join DMAC

No experience required

We teach the tools as we go, whether that's editing software, design programs, or the basics of coding and computer setup. Show up curious, leave with skills.

Build a real portfolio

Work on projects you can point to later, for college applications, job applications, or just to have something to show for your time.

Shared tools & resources

Members pitch in extra gear, drives, cables, and software tips so everyone has what they need to get started.

Hands-on, not just talk

Meetings are built around making things, not sitting through slides.

Find your people

Work alongside other students into the same stuff, at whatever level you're at.

Get your work seen

Featured in our projects gallery and at school events.

Game design and audio production are on our radar as the club grows — let us know if that's something you'd want to see.

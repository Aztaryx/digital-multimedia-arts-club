FAQ FAQ

Does your team work anywhere else? Do you do commissions?

No — DMAC is a school-based club, not a freelance team. We operate exclusively at Governor Ferrer Memorial Integrated National High School, located in Pinagtipunan, General Trias, Cavite. We don't take on outside projects or paid commissions.

What are the minimum requirements for joining?

Head over to our How to Join page for the full rundown on the sign-up process and what you'll need to prepare.

Where else can I follow DMAC for updates?

We're on Facebook! Check out our Socials page for the link and stay in the loop on what we're up to.

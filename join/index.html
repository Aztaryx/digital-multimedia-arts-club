How to Join How to Join
Step 1

Enroll at Our School

Before you can join DMAC, you need to be an officially enrolled student at Governor Ferrer Memorial Integrated National High School. If you're new to the school, here's the standard DepEd enrollment process.

Grade 7 — Elementary Graduates
  • SF9 / Form 138 (Elementary Report Card showing promotion eligibility)
  • PSA Birth Certificate — Original & Photocopy
Transferees — From Another High School
  • SF9 / Form 138 (Latest report card from your previous school)
  • PSA Birth Certificate — Original & Photocopy
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character (from your old school)

Your SF10 / Form 137 (Permanent Record) will be requested directly by our school registrar from your previous school once you begin classes.

Online Pre-Registration

Keep an eye on the official school Facebook page at the start of enrollment season. You'll fill out the digital Enhanced Basic Education Enrollment Form (EBEEF) via the Google Form link posted there.

On-Site Document Submission

Bring your physical requirements to campus during the scheduled enrollment days for your grade level. Place everything in a long brown envelope and submit to the assigned Teacher-in-Charge.

Get Your Section

Once approved by school authorities, your details are logged into the Learner Information System (LIS). Final sectioning lists will be posted after processing.

Step 2

Join the Club

Once you're a student at the school, joining DMAC is straightforward. Reach out to us by email to get the application form and for any other inquiries.
